Dispel ROI Calculator Reference Notes

Compact ROI calculator reference distilled from the Dispel example for frontend layout comparison.

Useful Patterns

  • Primary calculator content appears immediately, not below a hero.
  • Inputs are grouped into short, repeated rows with clear labels.
  • Important output numbers are visually larger than supporting copy.
  • Secondary explanation is available without crowding first glance.

Avoid Copying

  • Heavy animation wrappers and generated class noise.
  • Inline styles and layout attributes from the raw export.
  • Large marketing sections that do not help the calculator task.
